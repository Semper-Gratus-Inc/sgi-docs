## 1. Scope of Services

SGI will provide software consulting and development services as described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under this Agreement. Each SOW is incorporated into this Agreement by reference. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, the SOW controls.

No work begins until both parties have signed the applicable SOW and SGI has received the required upfront payment.

## 2. Payment Terms

### 2.1 Project Fees
Setup fees are split 50% due before work begins and 50% due at launch, as specified in the SOW. Work does not begin until the upfront payment clears.

### 2.2 Monthly Retainer
Where applicable, monthly retainer fees are billed via Stripe on the 1st of each month. Retainer billing begins the month the project goes live. Monthly retainers continue until cancelled per Section 2.4.

### 2.3 Late Payment
Invoices not paid within 15 days of the due date accrue interest at 1.5% per month. SGI reserves the right to pause all active work until the account is brought current.

### 2.4 Cancellation
Either party may cancel a monthly retainer with 30 days written notice. Cancellation does not relieve the Client of outstanding balances.

### 2.5 Refunds
Upfront payments are non-refundable. If SGI terminates the engagement due to a material breach by Client, upfront fees already collected are retained. If SGI terminates without cause, upfront fees will be refunded pro-rated based on work completed.

## 3. Scope Changes

Any feature, requirement, or deliverable not described in the signed SOW is out of scope. Client-requested changes must be submitted in writing and are subject to a separate quote. No out-of-scope work begins without a written SOW amendment signed by both parties and, where applicable, an additional upfront payment.

Out-of-scope work is billed at $125–$150/hr as agreed in the SOW amendment.

## 4. Intellectual Property

### 4.1 Transfer on Final Payment
Upon receipt of all payments due under the applicable SOW, SGI assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in the custom code, designs, and deliverables created specifically for Client under that SOW.

### 4.2 SGI Retains
SGI retains ownership of all general frameworks, libraries, tools, templates, and pre-existing intellectual property incorporated into the deliverables. Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use these components as part of the delivered product.

### 4.3 Portfolio
SGI may display the completed work in its portfolio and marketing materials unless Client requests otherwise in writing within 30 days of launch.

## 5. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential all non-public information shared in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to business plans, credentials, client data, and technical systems. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

SGI will not share Client data, credentials, or system access with any third party without Client's prior written consent, except as required by law.

## 6. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

- Provide timely access to required systems, credentials, and information
- Designate a single point of contact authorized to approve deliverables
- Review and provide feedback within 5 business days of each deliverable submission
- Respond to questions within 1 business day

Delays caused by Client unavailability may extend the project timeline. SGI is not liable for missed deadlines caused by Client delays.

## 7. Warranties and Disclaimers

SGI warrants that the deliverables will function materially as described in the SOW for 30 days after launch. SGI will correct any defects reported in writing during this period at no charge.

EXCEPT AS STATED ABOVE, SGI PROVIDES ALL SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES "AS IS" AND DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

SGI's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client in the 3 months preceding the claim. In no event shall SGI be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice. Termination does not release Client from payment obligations for work completed or in progress.

SGI may terminate immediately and without notice if Client fails to pay a past-due invoice within 15 days of written notice, or materially breaches this Agreement.
