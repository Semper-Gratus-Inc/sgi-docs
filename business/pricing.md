# Pricing & Packaging

## Tiers

| Tier | Setup Fee | Upfront | Monthly | Best For |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|----------|
| Starter | $500 | $250 | $79/mo | Local service businesses, up to 5 pages |
| Business | $1,500 | $750 | $149/mo | Growing businesses, up to 10 pages + blog + SEO |
| Pro | $3,500 | $1,750 | $249/mo | Custom web application, portals, integrations |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Multi-location, complex scope, dedicated build |

## Add-Ons

| Add-On | Price |
|--------|-------|
| E-commerce store | +$800 setup / +$50/mo |
| Logo & branding | $300 one-time |
| Additional pages | $150/page |
| Monthly content updates | +$99/mo |
| Custom integrations / hourly | $125–$150/hr |

## Custom / Enterprise Scope

- Enterprise tier is for clients whose needs exceed the Pro tier
- Scoped individually via discovery call
- Price built around actual requirements — not a package

## Quoting Rules

- Never quote a price verbally without reviewing scope first
- Always use the SOW template to confirm scope before issuing an invoice
- Hourly work billed monthly in arrears with itemized time log
