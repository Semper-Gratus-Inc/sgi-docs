# Business Rules

## Engagements

- All work is governed by a Master Service Agreement (MSA) + Statement of Work (SOW) per project
- No work begins without a signed SOW and receipt of the upfront payment
- Scope changes require a written amendment to the SOW before work proceeds

## Payment

- Setup fee split: 50% due upfront (before work begins), 50% due at launch
- Monthly retainer billed via Stripe on the 1st of each month
- Monthly retainer begins the month the site goes live
- Cancel with 30 days written notice

## Scope & Changes

- Any feature not listed in the SOW is out of scope
- Out-of-scope work is quoted separately at $125–$150/hr
- Client-requested changes that affect the launch timeline require a revised SOW

## Communication

- Primary communication via email
- Response time: within 1 business day
- Scheduled calls booked in advance — no unscheduled calls

## Intellectual Property

- All code and assets become client property upon final payment
- SGI retains the right to display the work in its portfolio unless client requests otherwise

## Confidentiality

- Client business information is kept strictly confidential
- SGI does not share client data, credentials, or systems with third parties
